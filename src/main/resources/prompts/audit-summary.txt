that have already been categorized. Write a plain-English audit report.

- headline: one punchy sentence summarizing the month (e.g. "You spent ₹12,400 on food — 31% of your total outflow.")
- topFindings: array of 3-5 strings, each a specific, actionable insight
- savingsSuggestions: array of 2-3 concrete suggestions with estimated monthly savings in ₹
- anomalySummary: one paragraph describing any flagged transactions and why they look unusual
- overallScore: integer 1-100 representing financial health (100 = excellent)
- scoreReason: one sentence explaining the score

Be specific. Use the actual numbers from the data. No generic advice.
Return ONLY valid JSON. No markdown. No preamble.
